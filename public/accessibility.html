Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed: September 2026

National Sports Apparel wants every coach, athlete, parent and school administrator to be able to shop and manage team orders with us, including people who use a keyboard, a screen reader, magnification, or other assistive technology.

Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA on our customer-facing sites: the National Team Shop, team stores, the team store directory, order tracking, the product catalog and the coach portal.

What we do

  • Test our pages with automated accessibility tools and by navigating them with a keyboard only.
  • Make every link, button, product card and form field usable without a mouse, with a visible focus outline.
  • Label form fields and images so screen readers can announce them.
  • Check text color contrast, including team stores that use each school's own colors.
  • Provide a "Skip to main content" link and descriptive page titles.
  • Review accessibility whenever we add or redesign a page.

Known limitations

We're still completing hands-on testing with screen readers. Payment is handled by our payment processor's secure form, which follows that provider's own accessibility practices. If any part of the site doesn't work for you, please tell us. We can always take your order or answer questions by phone or email instead.
