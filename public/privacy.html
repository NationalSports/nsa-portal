NSA Connect privacy information

National Sports Apparel uses account and contact information to provide staff access, process apparel orders, support customers, and maintain company business records.

Service providers

Supabase provides account authentication and database services. Netlify hosts the portal and server functions. Stripe processes payments for physical apparel orders. Configured error monitoring uses Sentry.

Optional AI assistance

When you allow AI assistance, your messages and recent chat history are sent to Anthropic to generate replies. The staff assistant also receives screen context and the open record name. The Team Shop assistant may receive order information needed to answer your question. Avoid entering passwords or payment details. You can turn off AI in the chat. Turning it off stops future AI chat requests; it does not erase information already sent. This disclosure covers these chat features; other internal automation must be reviewed separately before mobile release.

Accounts and deletion

Staff can start an account deletion request from Account and privacy. Requests require processing; submitting one does not immediately delete the account. Personal account data and company records are reviewed separately. Orders, invoices and other records that must be retained remain subject to applicable business and legal requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions or an existing deletion request, contact info@nationalsportsapparel.com.
